## 💰 Phase 2: Setting Up Your Bank Account & Monetization

### 1. Google AdSense (Monthly Passive Income to Bank)
* Once your site is live on Vercel, go to [adsense.google.com](https://adsense.google.com) and click **Get Started** ($0 registration).
* Enter your site URL (`omnicraft-ai.vercel.app`).
* Once approved, copy your AdSense script tag and paste it into the designated ad containers in `index.html`.
* **Bank Setup**: Inside AdSense, go to **Payments → Add Payment Method**. Type your **Bank Account Number, IFSC Code, and Bank Name**. When your earnings reach $100 (~₹8,300), Google automatically transfers money straight to your bank account every month!

### 2. Direct UPI Donations (For Indian Users)
* In `index.html`, open the donation modal section and replace `omnicraft@upi` with your real **GPay / PhonePe / Paytm UPI ID**.
* Every user who loves your free tool can send instant UPI money straight to your bank account!

### 3. BuyMeACoffee (For International Users)
* Create a free page on [buymeacoffee.com](https://buymeacoffee.com) ($0 cost).
* Connect your bank account.
* Paste your link inside the **"Support via BuyMeACoffee"** button in `index.html`.
